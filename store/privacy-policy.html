Animal Tap – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Animal Tap is a simple game for young children: tap an animal to hear its sound and see it move. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. Animal Tap does not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal information. It has no user accounts, and it does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts, or any other data.

Internet, ads and tracking

The app does not use the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party software development kits (SDKs), no in-app purchases, and no links that lead out of the app.

Permissions

Animal Tap does not request any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

The only thing the app saves is which colour theme was chosen, so it stays the same next time. This setting stays on the device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children's privacy

Animal Tap is designed for children. Because it collects no information at all, it does not collect personal information from children, and it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
